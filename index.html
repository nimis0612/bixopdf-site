3

OCR that we trained further ourselves

We started from open-source recognizers and kept training them on our own data. On a scanned book in Korean BixoPDF made about a third of the errors of leading PDF program A and a fifth of program B's. On a book in English it matched the better of the two. It all runs on your PC, so your documents never go to a server.

How we tested. Pages of born-digital PDFs were turned into 300-dpi greyscale scans with slight tilt, blur, noise and JPEG compression, saved as image-only PDFs, and given to each program's OCR on the same PC in October 2026. Each result's text layer was read the same way and compared with the source text, spaces left out. Neither book was used to train our recognizers. Programs A and B are two widely used commercial PDF editors with built-in OCR, at the versions installed on the test PC.

Disclaimer. These results come from tests run in a limited environment on a limited set of documents. They do not mean that the same results will be obtained in every environment or with every document. Accuracy depends on the scan, the layout, the fonts, the language and the settings used, and your results may differ.

Bookmarks and contents links, made for you

A 1,000-page scan is only useful if you can get around it. BixoPDF reads the book's own contents pages and builds the bookmark tree and the links for you. You check it and correct the few lines it is unsure of.

  1. Point to the contents pages

    Pick them in the page list, for example pages 5–12.

  2. It reads every entry

    Title, level from the numbering, and printed page number. Printed pages are matched to PDF pages through an offset or the book's page labels, then checked against each page's text.

  3. Correct with a few clicks

    Doubtful lines are flagged. Change a level with + and −, fix a page number, include or skip a line.

  4. Make bookmarks and links

    Each contents line becomes a link to its chapter. The links work in other PDF readers too, and running it again replaces the old links instead of stacking new ones.

Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Does OCR need an internet connection?

No. The recognizers are installed with the app and run on your computer. Your documents are not uploaded anywhere.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, and Windows 11, on 64-bit PCs.

Will my bookmarks and links work in other PDF readers?

Yes. They are saved as standard PDF bookmarks and link annotations.

What happens to text an older OCR program left in my PDF?

If that text is hidden under the page image, BixoPDF replaces it with the new OCR text when you save. If the text is the visible content of the page, it asks you first.

Can I make the app open my PDFs by default?

Yes. In Windows Settings → Apps → Default apps, choose BixoPDF for .pdf files.

Where do I buy it, and how do updates arrive?

BixoPDF is sold on Microsoft Store. It is installed and kept up to date by the Store, on every Windows PC signed in with the same Microsoft account.

How do I get help?

Write to support@bixopdf.com. The support page lists what to include.
