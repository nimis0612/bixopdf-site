Paid once, not every month

Most PDF suites are sold by subscription, and a year of one costs several times BixoPDF's price. BixoPDF has one price, $29, with no tiers: every feature is in the full version. You buy it once on Microsoft Store, which installs it and keeps it up to date on every Windows PC signed in with the same Microsoft account. There is no sign-in inside the app, no cloud storage and no renewal reminders.

What it does

  • Bookmarks, annotations and memos. Highlight, underline, strike through, draw, add boxes, arrows, memos, text boxes, stamps and signatures. Every annotation is listed by kind and page; click one to go to it. They are saved as standard PDF annotations, so other readers show them. More
  • Pages. Insert, delete, move, rotate, crop and split pages, merge PDFs and images into one file, and set page labels like i, ii, 1, 2.
  • OCR on your own PC. Text recognition in 50 languages, all included, that runs on your computer, so documents are not uploaded. The result is an invisible text layer under the page image, so search and copy work in any PDF reader. Batch OCR handles a whole folder. More
  • Scan fixing. Thickness, sharpness, contrast and brightness for faint photocopies, with a live preview, applied to one page, a range or the whole book. Crop margins, split two-page spreads and straighten tilted pages. More
  • Bookmarks from the contents page. Point to a book's contents pages and BixoPDF builds the bookmark tree and the links; you correct the few lines it flags. More
  • Compression. Black and white, grayscale, JPEG quality and resolution, with a size estimate and a preview first. A 1,082-page scanned book went from 609 MB to 332 MB with its colour pages kept. More
  • A dictionary in seven languages. Double-click a word for its meaning: English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, German, French and Russian. Language packs are free and downloaded once. More
  • The rest. Search across a folder of PDFs, password protection with AES-256, pages saved as JPG or PNG, export to Word or Excel, watermarks, and a focus mode.

It is also quick with large files. A 3,286-page, 383 MB court report was on screen and ready to scroll in under a second on our own Windows 11 PC, and jumping to its last page took 0.2 seconds.

What it does not do

BixoPDF is deliberately smaller than a full PDF suite. Before you switch, check that you do not need any of these:

  • Form designers and print-production preflight are left out.
  • There is no sign-in, cloud storage or sync between devices. Your files stay where you keep them.
  • It is a Windows app only: Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11, on 64-bit PCs.

The easiest way to find out whether it covers your work is the trial. It is the full app, with no watermark and no limits, for 15 days from the day you install it. One trial is given per PC. The trial setup is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you; the Store version shows no warning.

Questions

Is BixoPDF a subscription?

No. BixoPDF costs $29, paid once on Microsoft Store. There are no tiers and no monthly bill, and every feature is included.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed and the app works offline, OCR included. The only download is a dictionary language pack, once, when you ask for it.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The 15-day trial version is the full app with no watermark and no limits. One trial is given per PC.

Will my annotations show in other PDF readers?

Yes. Bookmarks, annotations, memos and contents links are saved as standard PDF bookmarks and annotations.
