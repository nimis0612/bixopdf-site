Devanagari, with the rest of the line

Printed Hindi is rarely Devanagari alone. Reports, textbooks and government papers mix in figures, dates, English terms and abbreviations. BixoPDF's Devanagari recognizer reads Hindi, Marathi, Nepali and Sanskrit, with numbers and Latin words on the same line, so a mixed sentence comes out as one sentence. Pages set in two columns are read column by column, in the order you read them.

We trained our recognizers on our own data, and all 50 OCR languages are included in the one price.

How it works

  1. Open the scan. Open the PDF in BixoPDF. Old photocopies with thin, broken strokes can be made thicker and darker first, with a live preview. Tilted pages are straightened before reading.
  2. Run OCR. Start recognition from the OCR panel, with Hindi or another Devanagari language. It runs in the background, so you can open other PDFs in tabs and keep working. Batch OCR takes a whole folder of PDFs and makes all of them searchable.
  3. Check and save. Every recognized line is boxed on the page and listed beside it. When you save, the text is stored in the PDF as an invisible layer under the page image. The page looks unchanged, but search, selection and copy now work in BixoPDF and in any other PDF reader.

If an older program left weak OCR text hidden under the scan, BixoPDF replaces it with the new result. The text can also be exported as plain text, or the PDF saved as a Word document or Excel workbook with the OCR text included. Compressing a large scan afterwards keeps the text layer, so the smaller copy stays searchable.

OCR uses a lot of memory. On a PC with limited memory, save your work and let one OCR job finish before starting the next.

Offline and private

The recognizers are installed with the app and run on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, so records, contracts and manuscripts can be made searchable without sending them to an online OCR service, and it works where the connection is slow or absent.

Hindi in the app, too

BixoPDF's own interface is available in Hindi, and watermarks in Hindi are set as Windows shows them. Questions

Which Devanagari languages does BixoPDF read?

Hindi, Marathi, Nepali and Sanskrit, with numbers and Latin words on the same line.

Does Hindi OCR need an internet connection?

No. The recognizers are installed with the app and run on your computer. Your documents are not uploaded anywhere.

Is the app available in Hindi?

Yes. Hindi is one of the app's languages; switch to it under Settings → Language. OCR reads 50 languages whatever the app's language.

Do you publish an accuracy figure for Hindi?

Not yet. Our published comparisons are for a Korean and an English book. The 15-day trial lets you test Hindi OCR on your own scans.
